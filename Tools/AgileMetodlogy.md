### 🚀 Agile Methodologies

- **Scrum**: Iterative framework with defined roles, events, and artifacts for delivering value in sprints.
- **Kanban**: Visual workflow management using boards to limit work-in-progress and optimize flow.
- **Extreme Programming (XP)**: Focuses on technical excellence through practices like pair programming and continuous integration.

---

### 🗓️ Sprint Ceremonies

- **Sprint Planning**: Team plans work for the upcoming sprint based on priorities.
- **Daily Scrum**: Short daily meeting to sync progress and identify blockers.
- **Sprint Review**: Team showcases completed work to stakeholders for feedback.
- **Sprint Retrospective**: Reflects on the sprint to improve future processes.
- **Product Backlog Refinement**: Ongoing activity to clarify, estimate, and prioritize backlog items.

### 🧑‍🏫 Scrum Master
- **Role:** Facilitator of the Scrum process.
- **Responsibility:** Ensures the team follows Scrum practices, removes obstacles, and helps improve team dynamics.

### 👩‍💼 Product Owner
- **Role:** Voice of the customer.
- **Responsibility:** Manages the product backlog, sets priorities, and ensures the team delivers value to the business.

### 👥 The Team (Development Team)
- **Role:** Cross-functional professionals who build the product.
- **Responsibility:** Self-organize to plan, develop, test, and deliver product increments.
