# 📚 Influential Books on AI & The Future

## Top Recommendations
- **The Coming Wave** (2023) – Mustafa Suleyman  
  *Focus*: AI + synthetic biology as defining forces.  
  *Vision*: The “containment problem” of controlling powerful AI.  

- **AI 2041: Ten Visions for Our Future** (2021) – Kai-Fu Lee & Chen Qiufan  
  *Focus*: Sci-fi + analysis of AI’s impact.  
  *Vision*: Industry-by-industry look at healthcare, education, daily life.  

- **The Singularity Is Nearer** (2024) – Ray Kurzweil  
  *Focus*: Human-level AI by 2029.  
  *Vision*: Human-machine merging and exponential advances.  

- **Life 3.0: Being Human in the Age of AI** (2017) – Max Tegmark  
  *Focus*: Long-term societal impact.  
  *Vision*: Utopian vs dystopian AI futures.  

- **Co-Intelligence: Living and Working with AI** (2024) – Ethan Mollick  
  *Focus*: Practical guide to generative AI.  
  *Vision*: AI as a partner, not just a tool.
