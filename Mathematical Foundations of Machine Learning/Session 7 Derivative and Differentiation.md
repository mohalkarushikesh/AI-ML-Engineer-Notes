# 📘 Derivative and Differentiation Notes

## 🌱 Concept
- **Derivative** → Measures the *rate of change* of a function at a point (slope of the curve).  
- **Differentiation** → The process of finding derivatives.  

---

## 🔑 Methods
- **Delta Method (Limits)**  
  - Using limits, slope at a point is:  
    - $f'(x) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x+\Delta x) - f(x)}{\Delta x}$

---

## 📝 Differentiation Rules

- **Notation**  
  - $f'(x), \frac{dy}{dx}, Df(x)$ → all mean derivative of $f$.  

- **Power Rule**  
  - $\frac{d}{dx}(x^n) = n x^{n-1}$  

- **Constant Multiple Rule**  
  - $\frac{d}{dx}[c \cdot f(x)] = c \cdot f'(x)$  

- **Sum Rule**  
  - $\frac{d}{dx}[f(x) + g(x)] = f'(x) + g'(x)$  

- **Product Rule**  
  - $\frac{d}{dx}[f(x) \cdot g(x)] = f'(x)g(x) + f(x)g'(x)$  

- **Quotient Rule**  
  - $\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\right] = \frac{f'(x)g(x) - f(x)g'(x)}{[g(x)]^2}$  

- **Chain Rule**  
  - $\frac{d}{dx}[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$  

---

## 🎯 Summary
- Derivative = slope / rate of change.  
- Differentiation = process of finding derivative.  
- Rules make differentiation faster without using limits every time.
