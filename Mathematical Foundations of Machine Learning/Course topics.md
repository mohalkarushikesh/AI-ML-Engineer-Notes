# 🧠 Course 1st Mathematical Foundations of Machine Learning

## 📘 Course Title
**Essential Linear Algebra and Calculus Hands-On in NumPy, TensorFlow, and PyTorch**

---

## 📖 Overview

Mathematics forms the core of data science and machine learning. To become an effective data scientist, it is essential to have a working understanding of the most relevant mathematical concepts.

While high-level libraries such as **Scikit-learn** and **Keras** make it easy to get started, understanding the underlying mathematics unlocks far greater potential. It allows you to:

- Identify and debug modeling issues  
- Improve existing models  
- Design new and innovative algorithms  
- Increase your long-term impact as a data scientist
