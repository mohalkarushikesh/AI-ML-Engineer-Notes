**The video is a beginner-friendly crash course on Deep Learning. At a high level, it introduces what deep learning is, explains how neural networks work, covers essential terminology, explores different learning paradigms, and walks through common neural network architectures and the five steps to building a deep learning model.**

---

[Deep Learning Crash Course Youtube Link...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VyWAvY2CF9c)

## 📘 High-Level Topics Covered

### 1. **Introduction to Deep Learning**
- What deep learning is and how it fits within machine learning.  
- Why it matters in modern AI applications.  

### 2. **Neural Networks Basics**
- Structure of a neural network (layers, neurons, weights).  
- How neural networks learn through forward and backward propagation.  

### 3. **Core Terminology**
- **Activation Functions**: Sigmoid, ReLU, etc.  
- **Loss Functions**: Measure prediction error.  
- **Optimizers**: Algorithms like SGD, Adam for updating weights.  
- **Parameters vs Hyperparameters**: Trainable vs manually set values.  
- **Epochs, Batches, Iterations**: Training cycle concepts.  

### 4. **Types of Learning**
- **Supervised Learning**: Labeled data.  
- **Unsupervised Learning**: Clustering, dimensionality reduction.  
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Agent-environment interaction with rewards.  

### 5. **Regularization**
- Techniques to prevent overfitting (dropout, L2 regularization).  

### 6. **Neural Network Architectures**
- **Feedforward Networks**: Basic fully connected layers.  
- **Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)**: Sequence modeling.  
- **Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)**: Image recognition.  

### 7. **Steps to Build a Deep Learning Model**
1. **Gathering Data**  
2. **Preprocessing Data**  
3. **Training the Model**  
4. **Evaluating Performance**  
5. **Optimizing Accuracy**  

### 8. **Conclusion**
- Recap of deep learning fundamentals.  
- Encouragement to explore further with coding and projects.
