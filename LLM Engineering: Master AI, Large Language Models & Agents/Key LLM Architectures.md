**Here’s a concise list of major LLM (Large Language Model) architectures developed over the years:**  
They are mostly based on the **Transformer architecture**, but each introduces unique innovations.

---

## 🧠 Key LLM Architectures (2017–2025)

- **Transformer (2017)** – The foundation architecture introduced in *Attention Is All You Need*. Encoder-decoder design with multi-head self-attention.  
- **BERT (2018)** – Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers. Pretrained with masked language modeling for deep contextual understanding.  
- **GPT series (2018–2023)** – Generative Pretrained Transformers (GPT, GPT-2, GPT-3, GPT-4). Autoregressive models focused on text generation.  
- **XLNet (2019)** – Improves on BERT by using permutation-based language modeling for better sequence handling.  
- **T5 (2019)** – Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer. Frames all NLP tasks as text-to-text problems.  
- **Switch Transformer (2021)** – Uses mixture-of-experts layers for scaling efficiently.  
- **GLaM (2021)** – Google’s Generalist Language Model with sparse mixture-of-experts.  
- **Gopher (2021)** – DeepMind’s model emphasizing long-context understanding.  
- **Chinchilla (2022)** – Optimized scaling laws; smaller model but trained with more data for efficiency.  
- **LaMDA (2022)** – Google’s dialogue-focused model for conversational AI.  
- **PaLM & PaLM 2 (2022–2023)** – Pathways Language Model, highly scalable with advanced reasoning.  
- **InstructGPT / ChatGPT (2022)** – Fine-tuned GPT models aligned with human instructions.  
- **Claude (2023)** – Anthropic’s model emphasizing safety and constitutional AI.  
- **Gemini (2023–2024)** – Google DeepMind’s multimodal LLM integrating text, images, and more.  
- **LLaMA & LLaMA 2/3 (2023–2025)** – Meta’s lightweight, open-source models optimized for efficiency.  
- **Mistral (2023)** – Efficient open-weight model with strong performance at smaller scale.  
- **Qwen-1.5 (2024)** – Alibaba’s family of models with multilingual support.  
- **DeepSeek-R1 (2025)** – Recent architecture focusing on reasoning and efficiency.
