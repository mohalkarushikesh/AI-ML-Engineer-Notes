**Here’s a curated list of popular free datasets across major AI/ML sectors. These are widely used for learning, experimentation, and benchmarking, and they’re all openly available.**

---

## 📊 Computer Vision
- **MNIST** – Handwritten digit images (28x28 grayscale).  
- **CIFAR-10 / CIFAR-100** – Small color images across 10 or 100 classes.  
- **ImageNet (subset)** – Large-scale dataset of labeled images for object recognition.  
- **COCO (Common Objects in Context)** – Images with object detection, segmentation, and captioning annotations.  
- **Open Images Dataset** – Millions of annotated images for detection and classification.

---

## 🗣️ Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- **IMDB Reviews** – Sentiment analysis dataset of movie reviews.  
- **SQuAD (Stanford Question Answering Dataset)** – Reading comprehension dataset with Q&A pairs.  
- **AG News** – News articles categorized into 4 classes.  
- **WikiText** – Large language modeling dataset from Wikipedia.  
- **TREC Question Classification** – Dataset of open-domain questions classified into categories.

---

## 🧪 Healthcare & Bioinformatics
- **MIMIC-III** – Clinical database with de-identified health records (requires credentialed access).  
- **COVID-19 Open Research Dataset (CORD-19)** – Research papers related to COVID-19.  
- **Breast Cancer Wisconsin Dataset** – Diagnostic dataset for classification tasks.  
- **PhysioNet ECG Data** – Physiological signals for heart disease detection.  
- **Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO)**
